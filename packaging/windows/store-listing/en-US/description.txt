MyPDF (OG) is a native desktop PDF reader and editor for everyday document work. Read, annotate, edit, organize, fill, and sign PDFs in one focused workspace. Your documents are processed on your computer, with no account or cloud upload required.

READ COMFORTABLY
Open documents in compact tabs, drag to reorder them, and organize them into named groups such as Work or Personal. Find open files in the searchable tab list and use right-click actions to manage tabs and groups. The Windows taskbar Jump List provides compact group shortcuts, recent PDFs, and Open/New actions; choosing an open document returns to its existing tab. Navigate with thumbnails and outlines, search text, and choose the zoom and reading layout that suits your screen. Start a blank PDF whenever you need a fresh page. Use the compact File, Edit, View and other application menus to reach commands. Save updates the current file; Save As lets you choose another name.

MAKE IT YOUR WORKSPACE
Choose Graphite, Glacier or Calico styling and Selective accents, Color families or Soft duotone icons. Appearance choices keep the controls in their familiar positions. Slim accent sliders and precise number fields make values easier to adjust, including line and arrow head sizes; property panels keep their scrollbars clear of the controls. Signature settings are grouped together for easy management.

EDIT AND ANNOTATE
Add text directly on the page and select supported existing text or images automatically in Edit mode. Highlight, underline, use squiggly underlines or strike out passages, add notes and callouts, and draw with the pen or eraser. Choose grouped shapes, lines and arrows with stroke styles, pattern fills and independent start/end heads. Use standard stamps or your own SVG and image artwork. Move, resize, recolor, rotate, and flip supported objects. Save and manage reusable styles for different purposes.

WORK WITH TABLES
Create movable or embedded tables, edit cells, resize rows and columns, set colors and spacing, and apply built-in templates. Detect supported bordered tables in a selected area and detach them for editing.

SIGN AND FILL
Create a visual signature by drawing, typing, or importing an image. Add certificate-based digital signatures using a P12/PFX file or a supported Windows certificate-store identity, with sharp vector text and ink in the visible signature. Fill PDF forms, create form fields, and import or export form data.

ORGANIZE YOUR PAGES
Reorder, insert, delete, extract, merge, split, rotate, and crop pages. Export multiple PDF pages onto each sheet using preset or custom grids, with adjustable margins, headers, footers, and watermarks.

CONVERT AND KEEP WORK MOVING
Convert images to PDF and use the compact Export ribbon for Word, Text, Markdown and Image, with advanced options in the side panel. Export pages as PNG or JPEG. Use undo, safe saving, and local recovery copies while you work.

EVERYDAY WORKFLOWS
Reuse local document templates, batch-compress PDFs or add page numbers and watermarks, and prepare separate sharing copies with selected comments, attachments and metadata removed. Match nearby text styling and align objects using optional table and baseline guides.

TRANSLATE ON YOUR COMPUTER
Translate selected text or pages locally using downloadable language packs, with no cloud fallback. Read the result in the side panel or attach it as a note. Model downloads require internet access; document text stays on your computer.

LOCAL BY DESIGN
MyPDF has no advertising, usage analytics, or automatic document uploads. Settings, custom stamps, signatures, and recovery data stay in local application storage. You choose where to save and share your files.

Text editing depends on the PDF's structure and fonts. Markdown and Word conversions focus on text and may not preserve complex layouts. Certificate trust depends on the recipient's PDF viewer and trust settings. OCR is not included in the Windows version.
