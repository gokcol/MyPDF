New in MyPDF 2.1.4:

- Clearer annotation text, shapes and image artwork at reduced zoom.
- Stamps, checkmarks and saved ink signatures remember their chosen placement sizes.
- Improved page-thumbnail multi-selection and context actions, with page copy/paste between open PDFs.
- Thumbnail navigation preserves reading history; page exports protect unfinished edits.
- Removed the duplicate Find toolbar icon. Search remains in the sidebar and on Ctrl+F.
- Existing settings, saved artwork and font compatibility fixes are retained.
