New in MyPDF 2.1.3:

- Improved PDFs with nonembedded fonts: available installed substitutes restore Turkish and other supported characters.
- Corrected wrong letters caused by duplicate glyph names in substitute fonts.
- Viewing, thumbnails, text geometry and exports now share the same font fallback. Embedded PDF fonts and text positions remain unchanged.
- Includes the on-page text-box typing and background-color improvements from 2.1.2.
