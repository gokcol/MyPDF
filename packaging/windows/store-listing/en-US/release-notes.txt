MyPDF 3.0.0

• Touch controls: finger drawing on/off, two-finger pan and pinch, long-press menus, and a movable floating toolbar.
• Automatic touchscreen detection plus Touch and Mouse only modes; tune touch targets, hold delay, stroke smoothing, and gesture hints.
• Redesigned Home with tools, recents, favorites, and customizable section order and visibility.
• Polished Settings and About; contextual icon tabs, compact previews, Studio and Sage styles, and three icon-color choices.
• The left panel starts hidden; saved document layouts remain available.
• Click existing text to edit it. Clicking outside keeps changes and finishes editing; another click starts the next edit.
• A4 defaults for new pages; resize individual pages without scaling their contents. Inserted images always fit the page.
• Editable image layers: move, resize, rotate, add text above images, and reset rotation from the context menu.
• Insert PDF, images, TXT, Markdown, SVG, DOCX and RTF; Word or LibreOffice is needed for DOCX/RTF.
• Recent improvements retained: stable table-cell editing, compact text properties, editable PDF metadata, and reliable Save/autosave.
• OCR page selection and reviewable results; Windows bundles offline English, Turkish and German OCR.
• Offline What’s New; Store updates on Windows and signed Mac updates with download, install and relaunch.
