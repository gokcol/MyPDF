New in MyPDF 2.1.6:

- Copy PDF text with its font, size, bold/italic style and color into compatible apps.
- Avoid duplicate copied lines when PDFs draw the same text repeatedly to simulate bold.
- Recover damaged Latin ligature mappings when the matching font is available locally.
- Extend a text selection with Shift-click after releasing the mouse.
- Keep copied PDF images available for pasting into other apps.
- Existing settings and saved artwork are retained.
