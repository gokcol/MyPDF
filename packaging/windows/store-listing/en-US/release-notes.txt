MyPDF 3.1.0

• Move and resize annotations reliably; drag inside text boxes to move, double-click to type. Choose font family and regular, bold or italic styles.
• Stamps stay in Annotate with editable text and colors. Reuse custom variants or open the visual artwork editor.
• Visual stamp designer: select, move, resize, rotate and layer objects; edit text, shapes, colors and transparency with Undo/Redo.
• Six border templates, rounded corners and independent background, fill and outline opacity; advanced SVG source remains available.
• Touchscreen detection, Touch/Mouse modes, finger drawing, two-finger pan/pinch, long-press menus and a floating toolbar.
• Customizable Home, polished Settings/About, five visual styles and compact icon previews. Left panel starts hidden.
• Click existing text to edit; click outside to finish, then click again for the next edit. Improved table-cell editing.
• A4 new pages, per-page resizing and images fitted to the page; move/resize/rotate images and add text above them.
• Insert PDF, images, TXT, Markdown and SVG; DOCX/RTF use installed Word or LibreOffice.
• Choose OCR pages and review results; offline Windows OCR includes English, Turkish and German. Edit PDF metadata.
• Reliable Save/autosave, offline What’s New and update checks. Mac 3.1 uses a signed, notarized DMG download.
