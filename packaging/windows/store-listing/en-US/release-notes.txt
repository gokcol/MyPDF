New in MyPDF 2.2.1:

- Area Highlight creates a translucent rectangle without snapping to text. Find it at the end of Annotate → More.
- Underline, Squiggly, Strikeout and Table now live in the More menu for a simpler annotation toolbar.
- Area highlights preserve their appearance when saved and reopened, with undo and redo support.
- Retains optional Comfortable spacing, the compact Home screen, highlighted search results and thumbnails that follow page navigation from 2.2.
