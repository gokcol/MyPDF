New in MyPDF 2.2.0:

- A compact Home screen brings recent documents closer, with resume-page labels and more readable filenames.
- Annotation tools show labels when space allows; search results highlight matching words and the full row is clickable.
- Theme accents are consistent, repeated filenames have folder hints, and shortcut labels match your platform.
- Choose optional Comfortable spacing for larger controls; Compact remains the default.
- Thumbnails follow Up/Down and other page navigation while preserving manual browsing and page-action selections.
- Preserve the normal window-drag pointer, touchscreen scrolling, rich copying, settings and reading positions.
