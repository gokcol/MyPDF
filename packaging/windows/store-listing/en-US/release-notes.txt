MyPDF 2.1.1 improves the property-panel controls:

- Slimmer sliders and clearer handles for drawing, colors, signatures, images and page layouts.
- Tidier numeric fields with clearer focus and exact value entry.
- A head-size slider alongside the precise point-size field for lines and arrows.
- Property-panel scrollbars have their own gutter, keeping controls clear and the document width steady while scrolling.
- Start/end style dropdowns fit narrow inspectors cleanly.
